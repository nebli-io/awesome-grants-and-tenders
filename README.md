# Awesome Grants & Tenders

> A curated list of the essential official sources to find **public and private grants (ayudas, subvenciones) and public and private tenders (licitaciones)** — focused on Spain, with the main European lines and a few global references.

Maintained by [Nebli](https://nebli.io) — AI grant and tender finder for companies.

🇪🇸 [Versión en español](README.es.md)

Scope: official and primary sources only (government portals, official gazettes, funding agencies and a few major private foundations). No aggregators, no local-level portals.

## Spain – national
- [BDNS / Infosubvenciones](https://www.infosubvenciones.es) – National Database of Subsidies: calls and awards from all administrations.
- [BOE – Boletín Oficial del Estado](https://www.boe.es) – Official State Gazette.
- [Plataforma de Contratación del Sector Público](https://contrataciondelestado.es) – Public tenders.
- [CDTI](https://www.cdti.es) – R&D&I funding.
- [IDAE](https://www.idae.es) – Energy efficiency and renewables.
- [ICO](https://www.ico.es) – Official Credit Institute.
- [ENISA](https://www.enisa.es) – Participative loans for startups and SMEs.
- [ICEX](https://www.icex.es) – Internationalisation.
- [Red.es](https://www.red.es) – Digitalisation.
- [AEI – Agencia Estatal de Investigación](https://www.aei.gob.es) – Research grants.
- [ISCIII](https://www.isciii.es) – Health research.
- [MITECO](https://www.miteco.gob.es) – Ecological transition.
- [FEGA](https://www.fega.gob.es) – Agricultural funds (CAP).
- [FUNDAE](https://www.fundae.es) – Training for employment.
- [AECID](https://www.aecid.es) – Development cooperation.
- [OEPM](https://www.oepm.es) – Patents and trademarks.
- [Fundación Biodiversidad](https://fundacion-biodiversidad.es) – Environment and biodiversity.
- [Cámaras de Comercio](https://www.camara.es) – Chambers of Commerce programmes.

## European Union
- [EU Funding & Tenders Portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/home) – Horizon Europe, Erasmus+, LIFE, Digital Europe, Creative Europe, EU4Health.
- [TED – Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu) – EU public procurement.
- [EIC – European Innovation Council](https://eic.ec.europa.eu) and [EISMEA](https://eismea.ec.europa.eu).
- [Interreg SUDOE](https://interreg-sudoe.eu) · [POCTEFA](https://www.poctefa.eu) – Cross-border cooperation.
- [European Investment Bank](https://www.eib.org) – EIB financing.
- [Fondos Europeos (Spain)](https://www.fondoseuropeos.hacienda.gob.es) – Spanish EU funds hub.
